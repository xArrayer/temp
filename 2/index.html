Testing zone — scroll away

This long section exists purely so you can verify the page scrolls smoothly behind the glass header, and that the body correctly locks when the menu or search opens. Drag the window narrower to test the mobile layout, wider for desktop, and everything in between for tablet behavior.

When the menu is open, the latest video starts playing immediately and stops the moment you close the menu — no ghost audio in the background. The Description panel on the right has its own custom scrollbar: grab the tiny glowing circle and drag it like a volume slider, tap the rail to jump, or just scroll the text normally with touch/wheel.

The search panel filters the universal data file live as you type, matching titles (and tags, as a bonus). Each result shows its thumbnail, title, glass tag pills, and the two action buttons — Watch Video and Module — exactly like the real pages will.

All sample images and the embedded video are placeholders so everything works out of the box. Swap them for your real content by editing only the two marked files — the design system stays untouched.

Once you're happy with the preview, this exact header + menu + search block can be pasted into every future page of the site, and the same videos-data.js keeps them all in sync forever.
